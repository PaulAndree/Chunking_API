Humanism as Science:

the structure of harmony in music, health, knowledge, emotions and ethics.

Each complex structure emerges by one of two strategies: (i) actively and “spontaneously” by autonomous growth according to an intrinsic preprogramed plan, like for living organisms, or (ii) passively by being constructed according to the plan of an engineer external to the structure, like in manmade technologies. It is conceivable that a class of futuristic robots could emerge from a mixture of two strategies.

Construction by growth is particularly interesting nature’s evolutionary way to make also human beings; it proceeds by the paradigmatic branching growth of trees. When we map growth of living creature from the first cell (zygote) to finished organism, it grows like a tree! The final product (an animal) takes forms unlike trees but grows by bifurcation of successive cell divisions that we can see macroscopically as the branching structures of many trees.

To tackle the structure of knowledge we can first appreciate that the brain grows hundreds of billions of neurones also like a tree. But, unlike the usual trees with well separated branches, neuronal branches in the brain establish also horizontal inter-neuronal connections resulting in 3D networks.
